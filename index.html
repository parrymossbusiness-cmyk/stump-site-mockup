FAQ

Common Questions About Stump Grinding

Do you serve my area?+

Yes — we serve the entire Greater Atlanta area, including Atlanta, Marietta, Sandy Springs, South Fulton, Roswell, College Park, Dunwoody, Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Smyrna, Decatur, and East Point. Call or text (770) 543-8633 to confirm your address.

Are you insured?+

Yes. A&L Stump Grinding is insured on every job. We're also a veteran-owned and operated business — Jay is a Marine veteran who runs every job personally.

How much does stump grinding cost?+

Cost depends mainly on stump diameter, how many stumps you have, and site access. Multiple stumps on the same visit typically cost less per stump than a single stump booked on its own. We give free, honest estimates before any work starts, and as our reviews mention, we don't nickel-and-dime you for extra stumps found on-site.

How long does stump grinding take?+

Most residential jobs — even multiple stumps — are done in a single visit, often within a few hours. We're known for fast turnaround; several customers have had us out same-day or next-day after calling.

Do you grind large stumps or stumps in tight spaces?+

Yes to both. Our compact tracked equipment fits through standard gates and fences to reach backyard stumps other machines can't, and stump size has never been a reason to turn down a job — all sizes and locations are handled.
