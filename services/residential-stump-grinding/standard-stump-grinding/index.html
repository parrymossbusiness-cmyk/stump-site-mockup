Standard Stump Grinding in Alpharetta, GA

Stumps ground below grade so mowers and landscaping never have to work around them again.

Overview

Standard stump grinding is the process of mechanically cutting a tree stump down below ground level using a rotating cutting wheel, rather than digging it out or waiting for it to decay naturally. It's the most common service we provide, covering the vast majority of residential stumps — a tree that was removed, a stump that's been sitting in the yard for years, or a stump left behind by a storm.

Homeowners typically call for standard stump grinding when a stump has become a nuisance: it's in the way of mowing, it's an eyesore in an otherwise finished yard, or it's blocking a planned landscaping or hardscaping project. Unlike stump removal by digging, which tears up a wide area of surrounding lawn and can take a full day of manual labor, grinding clears the stump in place with minimal disturbance to the yard around it.

Our process starts with a quick on-site or phone assessment to confirm stump diameter and location, followed by a free, upfront price. On the day of the job, our tracked grinder positions over the stump and works the cutting wheel down and across the stump face in passes, reducing it to wood chips below the surrounding grade. We clear surface roots radiating from the stump as needed, then handle grindings and rough grading before we leave.

Most standard stump grinding jobs are completed in under an hour per stump, weather and access permitting, and we can typically schedule within the same week you call — sometimes the same day. We'll walk you through backfill and cleanup options as part of your free estimate so there's nothing left to figure out once the grinding is done.

Pricing for a standard stump comes down mainly to diameter and root spread, which we confirm before quoting rather than guessing off a phone description alone when it's a borderline size. There's no minimum charge that makes a single small stump not worth calling about, and no maximum size that makes a large one too much to handle — we've ground everything from a young tree's first stump to decades-old hardwoods.

FAQ

Standard Stump Grinding Questions

How deep is a standard stump ground?+

Standard grinding typically clears the stump several inches below the surrounding grade, enough for grass, seeding, and most landscaping. Deeper grinding is available on request for projects like patios or footings.

Do I need to be home during the grinding?+

Not necessarily, as long as we have clear access to the stump and can reach you by phone if anything comes up. Many customers just mark the stump location and go about their day.
