Multiple Stump Removal in Atlanta, GA

Two stumps or twenty — priced and handled as one job, not one invoice per stump.

Overview

Multiple stump removal covers any job where a property has more than one stump to grind, whether that's two stumps from separate trees removed over the years or a dozen left behind after a lot was cleared or a storm took down several trees at once. We price these jobs as a whole rather than stump by stump, which is one of the biggest reasons homeowners choose to knock out several stumps in a single visit instead of calling us back repeatedly.

This service comes up most often for homeowners who've been putting off dealing with stumps one at a time and decide to clear the whole property at once, for new homeowners inheriting several old stumps from a previous owner's landscaping, and for anyone clearing a lot for new construction or a major landscaping overhaul.

We walk the full property during the estimate to count and assess every stump, then quote one price for the group. On the day of the job, our tracked equipment moves between stump locations without needing to be trailered and repositioned repeatedly, which keeps the job moving efficiently even across a large yard. Grindings and cleanup are handled the same way as a single-stump job, just scaled to cover every location.

One of the things we're known for is not nickel-and-diming customers when we find more stumps than expected once we're on-site — on more than one job we've ground several extra stumps beyond what was originally quoted, at no additional charge, because the difference in time was minimal once the equipment was already there and set up.

If you're not sure how many stumps you have or where they all are — common on a property that's changed hands a few times — we're happy to walk the whole lot with you during the estimate and point out anything you may have missed, old tree rings included, before giving you one final number.

FAQ

Multiple Stump Removal Questions

Do you offer a discount for multiple stumps?+

Effectively, yes — we price multiple stumps together as one job rather than charging full price per stump, which usually works out lower per stump than booking them separately.

What if you find more stumps once you start?+

We'll let you know and, in many cases, include them in the same job at little to no extra cost rather than requiring a separate visit and separate charge.
