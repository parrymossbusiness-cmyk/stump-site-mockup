Residential Stump Grinding in Atlanta, GA

Single stumps, multiple stumps, any size — ground flush and ready for whatever's next.

Overview

A stump left in the yard after a tree comes down isn't just an eyesore — it's a tripping hazard, a mower obstacle, and often the one thing standing between you and new sod, a fence line, or a finished landscaping project. A&L Stump Grinding grinds residential stumps of every size across the Atlanta metro, from a single stump in the corner of the yard to a full property's worth left over from a storm or a tree removal crew that only handled the tree.

Every job starts the same way: Jay walks the site, confirms stump size and count, and gives you a straightforward price before any equipment comes off the trailer. Our tracked stump grinder cuts the stump down below grade, chewing through the wood and surface roots so the area can be backfilled, seeded, or landscaped over without a stump edge catching a mower blade for years afterward.

Multiple stumps are common, and they don't have to mean multiple invoices. When homeowners have several stumps on the same property, we price the job as a whole rather than stump by stump — on one job we ground five stumps and found three more once we started, all for the price we quoted for five. That's the kind of thing you only find out from experience, not from a script.

This page covers our two most-requested residential grinding services, Standard Stump Grinding and Multiple Stump Removal, each detailed on its own page. Small Stump Removal, Stump Grinding for New Landscaping, and Root Flare Grinding are covered in the sections below. Whatever you're dealing with, we serve homeowners throughout Atlanta, Marietta, Sandy Springs, South Fulton, Roswell, College Park, Dunwoody, and the surrounding communities, and every estimate is free.

Additional Residential Stump Grinding Services

Is It Worth Calling a Pro for Just One Small Stump?

Yes, and it's usually more affordable than homeowners expect. A small stump might look like a weekend DIY project, but even a stump under a foot across has a root system extending well beyond what's visible above ground, and rental grinders are heavy, awkward, and not cheap for a single afternoon of use.

We grind small stumps as efficiently as larger ones, and since we're often already in the neighborhood for other jobs, a single small stump can frequently be scheduled quickly without a long wait. There's no minimum job size that makes it not worth calling.

If you've got one small stump you've been putting off dealing with, it's worth a quick call just to hear the price — it's often lower than the cost of renting equipment yourself, with none of the labor or cleanup.

Planning New Landscaping? Grind the Stump First

If you're planning new sod, a garden bed, a paver path, or any landscaping project in an area with an old stump, grinding it out first saves you from working around a problem that only gets more expensive to fix later. A stump left in place under new sod will eventually decay, sink, and leave a depression in your lawn — or send up new sprouts if the roots are still alive.

We grind deep enough that landscaping fabric, topsoil, and sod can go right over the area without the stump interfering, and we can coordinate timing with your landscaper or grade the area ourselves if you're handling the planting yourself.

This is one of the most common reasons homeowners call us before a spring landscaping project — getting the stump out early means one less unknown once the new plants or sod go in.

What Is Root Flare Grinding and Do You Need It?

The root flare is the widened base of a tree where the trunk transitions into the major roots, often spreading a foot or more beyond the visible stump itself. Standard stump grinding clears the stump and its immediate base, but a wider root flare sometimes needs additional grinding passes to fully clear, especially if the area needs to be completely flat for a patio, driveway extension, or paver installation.

We check root flare spread as part of the estimate, particularly on mature trees, and let you know upfront if a stump will need extra grinding width to clear it completely rather than discovering it mid-job.

If you're planning any hardscaping directly over or immediately next to an old stump, mention it when you call so we can plan for full root flare grinding rather than a standard grind.

Stump size factors into the estimate, but we quote the price upfront after seeing or hearing a clear description of the stump — no size has ever been a reason we've turned down a job.

How many stumps can you grind in one visit?+

As many as you need. Multiple stumps on the same property are priced together, and we've had jobs where we found additional stumps once we started and ground them at no extra charge.
