Our Story

A Marine Veteran Who Shows Up When I Say I Will

A and L Stump Grinding is an owner operated stump grinding company serving the Greater Atlanta area. My name is Jayson Schofield, a proud Marine veteran, and I've spent more than 10 years grinding residential stumps across north Georgia, everything from a single backyard stump squeezed between a flower bed and a fence to a full yard of storm-damaged trees.

My reputation is built one yard at a time. Customers mention the same things again and again in their reviews: I show up fast, sometimes within the hour; I work carefully around landscaping instead of tearing through it; and I don't pad the bill when a job turns out to be bigger than expected. That's the standard every job gets, whether it's one stump or ten.
